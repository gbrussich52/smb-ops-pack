# Your price sheet: how to set it up (about 20–40 minutes, once)

Your bot writes estimates from YOUR prices. It never makes one up.

1. Install the Front Desk Ops bot template in your own Grok Bot account.
2. Say: "Set up my price book."
3. Pick Google Sheets (it goes in your own Google Drive, private to you) or Excel (you get a file to keep).
4. Give it your items, whichever way is easiest:
   - paste or forward a supplier invoice or price list,
   - tell it out loud ("high hats: I pay $18, 40% markup, 45 minutes each"),
   - or type them into the sheet yourself.
5. Add your labor rates (e.g. Journeyman $95/hr), sales tax, and minimum job charge. Mark permits and fees "N" for not taxed.
6. Add other names you say for things ("can light", "pot light"), so it understands you.
7. It runs a small test estimate. Check the math and correct anything.

After that, just dictate the walk-through: "10 high hats, 200 ft 12/2, 2 20-amp circuits, permit."
You get a draft with every line's math, plus questions about anything it didn't find. Nothing goes to a customer until you say yes.
Works for any trade: electrical, masonry, plumbing, HVAC, landscaping...
